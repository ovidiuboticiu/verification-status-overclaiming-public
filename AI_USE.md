# AI assistance disclosure

This project was developed with **substantial AI assistance**.

AI tools, including OpenAI ChatGPT, were used for tasks including methodological discussion, protocol-design assistance, coding and code review, analysis checks, adversarial critique, and documentation.

AI outputs were not treated as an automatic source of authority. The human author reviewed, questioned, and worked through generated analyses and proposals before making the corresponding research decisions.

The human author retained responsibility for:

- selecting the research question;
- approving experimental designs, frozen gates, and stopping rules;
- authorizing and executing runs;
- interpreting the evidence;
- deciding when the project should stop;
- deciding what claims and artifacts should be published.

The reported behavioral observations and numerical results derive from the executed frozen protocols, model outputs, and recomputation tools, not from AI-generated substitute data. AI is not listed as an author.
